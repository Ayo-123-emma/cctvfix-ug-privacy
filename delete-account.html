CCTVFix UG Account Deletion

CCTVFix UG, operated by Emma Net, allows customers to create accounts for booking services, shopping, managing orders, and accessing other customer features.

If you no longer want to use your CCTVFix UG account, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data using the instructions below.

How to request account deletion

Send an account deletion request to the CCTVFix UG support email address using the email address associated with your CCTVFix UG account.

Use the subject: CCTVFix UG Account Deletion Request

In your request, provide:

  • Your full name
  • The email address registered with your CCTVFix UG account
  • Your phone number, if it is associated with the account

Do not send your password.

Data that may be deleted

After your request is verified and processed, data associated with your CCTVFix UG customer account may be deleted, including:

  • Customer profile information
  • Name, email address and phone number associated with the account
  • Saved customer account information
  • Wishlist or favorite information associated with the account
  • Other personal information associated with the customer account where deletion is applicable

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legitimate business, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, transaction records, or legal and regulatory obligations.

This may include limited records relating to completed service bookings, orders, transactions, returns, refunds, or other records that must be retained for legitimate or legal purposes.

Retained information will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Processing your request

CCTVFix UG may verify that the deletion request belongs to the account owner before deleting the account and associated data.

Once the request has been verified, we will process the deletion request within a reasonable period and notify you when it has been completed.
